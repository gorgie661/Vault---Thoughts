### Airline stopover programs 
1. If flying into Europe through Iceland air you can stay in the capitol for 7 days no extra charge
2. Turkish airlines will cover a 3 day hotel stay in Istanbul - good for flying internationally 
3. Fiji airways lets you stay in Nadi for up to 3 days if flying to Australia or NZ
4. Japan airlines lets you stay in Osaka or Tokyo for no extra charge for 7 nights 
5. UAE pays for a luxurious 3 nights stay if you have a long layover
>[!info] 
>Airline stopover programs are ways to stay in countries on layovers with no extra charge
