Strange that there’s a brain in there telling them what to do. Strange to think that there is a whole brain in their talking to them. Strange there is a soul 

People will follow a leader if the leader preaches on real issues. They know they should put effort into listening and participating, if only to impress their peers and maintain status. However if even a single person expresses indifference or disregard for the issue it inspires others to think freely. A leader gets stripped of followers by a leader. The lion devours the shepherd's sheep and will birth a kind of its own capable of the same.
