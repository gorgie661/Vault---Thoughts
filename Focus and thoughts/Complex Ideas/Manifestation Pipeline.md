The manifestation pipeline is the idea that through a growth mindset, positive thought, and dedication to betterment you create minor perception changes thus conditioning your body to make beneficial change to reach your goals creating the illusion of manifestation

In other words, when you want something to happen and it does it is because your body was conditioned to respond to certain thigs in a certain manor to make what you want happen.

It connects to the question of 'What controls your thoughts?'
Your subconscious perception to the environment controls your thoughts and thus actions. when you truly want something your subconscious picks up on that and will then interpret the environment in a certain way to produce thoughts that produce the most efficient and correct actions for you to reach your goal. 

Your body knows what you want and can pick up on tiny details and hints in the environment to inhibit thoughts that produce actions that lead you to what you want creating the illusion of manifestation. you got what you want only because your body found the correct path to it
