The Personal Validation Theory - PVT - is the theory that you can achieve certain mental aspirations just my gaslighting yourself and telling yourself that you posses those traits and aspirations. Its a combination of placebo, the [[Manifestation Pipeline]] and [[The Perception Alteration Theorem]] where your perception of yourself can be solely dictated by you and thus when you act like you are a certain way you become that way. Furthermore, simply acting a certain way can make others believe you are that way making others perception of you validate yourself
