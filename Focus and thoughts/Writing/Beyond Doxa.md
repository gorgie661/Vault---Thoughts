### Outline

Nano bot mental controllers in the food to control - in the dystopian future the government places nano tech into the food that controls people’s thoughts.
The people believe society is a beautiful utopia with infinite food and everything provided their emotions and perceptions are manipulated so people literally see things differently 
Society is so tightly controlled that the government puts nano bots in the food and water to control people and since people always have to eat they are always being controlled. The story is from the perspective of a man who suffered a cranial injury that breaks his nano bots and he sees the world how it really is for the first time. He realizes what the government has done and attempts to free people. He frees a lot of people and they rise up and try to overthrow the government. At the very end when face to face with the ruler he believes is some tyrannical powerful horrible person, Is a soulless calculating AI that just detonates the remaining nanobots in the revolutionaries systems and just kills them instantly (the idea incomprehensible power and total control is total)
