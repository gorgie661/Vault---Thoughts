### Story Writing 

The wind came in lazy gusts, gently caressing the tips of the dunes, stealing but a few granules of sand only to cast them out to flitter and fall across the untouched mounds. Peaceful. The surface of Alpa was once coated in the essence of humanity. Enormous structures soaring from the earth below. The home of a hundred billion human souls. From slaves to the emperor himself called Alpa their home. Now it was nothing but a dirty rock floating in the endless cosmos.

Six thousand years ago humanities expansion was astronomical. They had achieved colonization of the last frontier. Two thousand planets; trillions of lives all answering to one man: The emperor. A man whose name was long ago lost to the deafening silence of the universe. Humanity reached a point of such vast outreach it was impossible to regulate. Trade networks, politics, communication, all stretched to their technological limit. A solution was needed. At the emperor's command the very best and brightest among humanity created the Logistics and Optimization Governance Operating System - LOGOS. An artificial mind of immense intellect and presence, built into the crust of Alpa itself, connected to a trillion lives. LOGOS was given a simple task: Optimize humanity for the benefit of all humans; do what is necessary to benefit all of humanity. LOGOS obeyed.

Far in the distance the lights of ships began to dot the horizon. They were coming. The Ecologists, LOGOS called them. The solution to all of humanity's woes.The ships began to land all along the dusty surface of the planet. Thousands of Ecos poured out.  Faceless sexless things. Devoid of life or soul. Massive black things. They stand hunched over supported by  three enormous stumps. Gracing their shoulders in the cleft  where once a head would sit and observe the world, now exists a smooth bulging lump, the silhouette broken only by a monstrous tentacle filled maw. Grotesque twisted branches, a cynical homage to arms, sprouted from their chest, carrying the tools of their craft. Placed along their bent hunched spines bloom rows of fleshy vents. fungal membranes, and translucent sacs dot their trunks. Oozing with the seeds of new life. They walk hunched, supporting their vast bulging undercarriage filled with the furnace of their toil. They wander slowly, devoid of recollection, consciousness, victims of a logical design, fascinated only by their purpose. 

Far in the distance a particularly large specimen could be seen trodding around in search of organic material to start its process, when suddenly it seemed to disappear into the cosmos of sand below, fallen into the endless shafts and tunnels that made up the bulk of LOGOS’s complex.
